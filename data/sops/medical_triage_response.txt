MASS CASUALTY MEDICAL TRIAGE PROTOCOL
Standard Operating Procedure

1. TRIAGE SETUP
- Establish triage in a safe cold zone with clear ambulance access and patient flow.
- Use simple categories: immediate, delayed, minor, expectant/deceased.
- Tag every patient with identity if known, triage category, time, vital signs, and destination.
- Re-triage regularly because patient condition can change.

2. LIFE-SAVING INTERVENTIONS
- Control major bleeding, open airway, support breathing, and treat shock.
- Prioritize patients with airway compromise, severe bleeding, altered consciousness, burns, crush injury, or hypothermia.
- Keep walking wounded separate but supervised; they may deteriorate.
- Maintain infection prevention, PPE, and safe sharps disposal.

3. TRANSPORT COORDINATION
- Distribute patients across hospitals according to capacity and specialty.
- Notify receiving hospitals with patient count, injury type, ETA, and decontamination status.
- Keep one officer responsible for ambulance loading and destination decisions.

4. DOCUMENTATION AND FAMILY SUPPORT
- Maintain patient tracking from scene to hospital.
- Establish missing persons desk and family information channel.
- Arrange psychological first aid for survivors, families, and responders.
