EVACUATION, SHELTER, AND RELIEF COORDINATION PROTOCOL
Standard Operating Procedure

1. EVACUATION DECISION
- Evacuate when life safety risk exceeds the risk of movement.
- Define who must leave, where they should go, which routes are safe, and when movement starts.
- Prioritize hospitals, schools, elderly residents, persons with disabilities, and low-mobility households.
- Keep routes clear for emergency vehicles and provide transport for people without vehicles.

2. SHELTER OPERATIONS
- Register arrivals, identify medical needs, and separate families only when medically necessary.
- Provide minimum supplies: safe drinking water, food, blankets, lighting, hygiene kits, and charging points.
- Maintain sanitation, waste disposal, handwashing, menstrual hygiene, and safe spaces for children and women.
- Track shelter capacity and publish locations through official channels.

3. RELIEF DISTRIBUTION
- Match supplies to verified needs and avoid duplicate distribution.
- Prioritize water, food, medicine, baby supplies, assistive devices, and temporary shelter material.
- Keep transparent records of stock, dispatch, receipt, and gaps.

4. RETURN AND CLOSURE
- Permit return only after hazard clearance, utility safety checks, and route confirmation.
- Communicate residual risks, compensation process, health advisories, and complaint channels.
