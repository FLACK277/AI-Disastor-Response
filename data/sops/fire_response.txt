FIRE EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROTOCOL
National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) — Standard Operating Procedure

1. IMMEDIATE RESPONSE (0–15 Minutes)
- Alert nearest fire station via 101 emergency number.
- Activate building fire alarm and PA system for evacuation.
- Deploy first-response fire tenders within 7 minutes (urban) / 20 minutes (rural).
- Establish incident command post upwind of the fire.
- Request police for traffic control and perimeter security.

2. EVACUATION
- Initiate floor-by-floor evacuation starting from the fire floor and above.
- Use designated fire escape routes — never use elevators.
- Assembly point must be at least 100 meters from the structure.
- Conduct headcount using building occupant register.
- Deploy aerial ladders for high-rise rescues (floors above 4th).
- Use smoke hoods and breathing apparatus for rescuers entering smoke-filled areas.

3. FIREFIGHTING OPERATIONS
- Assess fire type: Class A (solid), B (liquid), C (gas), D (metal), E (electrical).
- Use appropriate extinguishing agent: water, foam, CO2, dry chemical powder.
- For electrical fires: cut power supply BEFORE applying water.
- Establish water supply from nearest hydrant or tanker relay system.
- Deploy thermal imaging cameras to locate trapped persons and hidden fire pockets.
- Maintain collapse zone perimeter — no personnel within distance equal to building height.

4. MEDICAL RESPONSE
- Deploy ambulances with burn treatment capability.
- Set up triage point at assembly area: Red (critical), Yellow (serious), Green (minor).
- Treat burn injuries: cool with clean water for 20 minutes, cover with sterile dressing.
- Monitor for smoke inhalation: administer oxygen, prepare for intubation if needed.
- Transfer critical patients to designated burn centers (list maintained by district).

5. HAZARDOUS MATERIALS
- If industrial chemicals involved, identify substance via Safety Data Sheet (SDS).
- Establish hot zone (immediate danger), warm zone (decon), cold zone (safe).
- Deploy HAZMAT team with chemical-resistant PPE.
- Set up decontamination shower for exposed persons.
- Monitor air quality downwind for toxic fumes.

6. INVESTIGATION AND RECOVERY
- Preserve fire scene for forensic investigation after suppression.
- Document fire origin, spread pattern, and contributing factors.
- Assess structural integrity before allowing re-entry.
- Provide temporary shelter for displaced occupants.
- Begin insurance documentation and loss assessment.
- Conduct critical incident stress debriefing for responders.

7. PREVENTION MEASURES
- Verify fire NOC compliance for all commercial and high-rise buildings.
- Conduct quarterly fire drills in schools, hospitals, and offices.
- Ensure functioning fire extinguishers on every floor.
- Maintain clear access roads for fire tenders (minimum 6m width).
- Install smoke detectors and sprinkler systems as per NBC 2016 standards.
