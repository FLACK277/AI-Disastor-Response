EARTHQUAKE EMERGENCY RESPONSE PROTOCOL
National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) — Standard Operating Procedure

1. IMMEDIATE RESPONSE (0–1 Hour)
- Activate Emergency Operations Center (EOC) immediately upon seismic event detection above magnitude 4.5.
- Issue automated alerts to all registered emergency responders within 100km radius.
- Deploy NDRF battalions from nearest staging areas within 30 minutes.
- Establish communication links with district administration and local police.
- Activate search-and-rescue (SAR) teams with equipment pre-positioned at district level.

2. SEARCH AND RESCUE PHASE (1–72 Hours)
- Conduct rapid damage assessment using aerial survey (drones/helicopters).
- Prioritize collapsed structures in high-density residential and commercial zones.
- Deploy canine units and acoustic detection equipment for trapped survivors.
- Set up triage centers at safe open areas near affected zones.
- Coordinate with military for heavy lifting equipment if structural collapse exceeds local capacity.
- Maintain 24/7 SAR operations with rotating shifts of 8 hours.

3. MEDICAL RESPONSE
- Activate mass casualty incident (MCI) protocol at all hospitals within 50km.
- Deploy Mobile Medical Units (MMUs) to areas where hospitals are damaged.
- Ensure blood bank inventory is verified and cross-matched within 2 hours.
- Set up psychological first aid stations alongside medical triage.
- Establish patient tracking system across all treatment facilities.

4. SHELTER AND RELIEF
- Open pre-identified shelter locations: stadiums, community halls, school grounds.
- Provide emergency supplies: water (3L/person/day), food, blankets, hygiene kits.
- Ensure separate facilities for women, children, elderly, and persons with disabilities.
- Set up temporary sanitation facilities (1 toilet per 50 persons).
- Begin damage assessment of residential structures for habitability classification.

5. COMMUNICATION
- Issue public advisories via SMS, radio, TV, and social media every 2 hours.
- Establish missing persons helpline and online portal.
- Provide multilingual updates (Hindi, English, regional languages).
- Brief media twice daily at designated press centers.
- Coordinate with telecom providers to restore network priority for emergency calls.

6. AFTERSHOCK MANAGEMENT
- Monitor seismic activity continuously; issue aftershock warnings.
- Evacuate damaged structures preemptively if aftershock risk is high.
- Re-inspect all bridges, flyovers, and critical infrastructure after aftershocks above magnitude 3.0.

7. RECOVERY PHASE (72 Hours Onward)
- Begin structural safety audits of all buildings in affected area.
- Initiate emergency housing assistance and insurance claim processing.
- Restore water supply, electricity, and gas connections after safety verification.
- Open schools and offices in verified-safe structures.
- Conduct post-event psychological support programs for affected communities.
