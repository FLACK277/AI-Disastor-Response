TSUNAMI RESPONSE PROTOCOL
Standard Operating Procedure

1. WARNING AND EVACUATION
- Evacuate coastal lowlands immediately after official tsunami warning, strong earthquake, or sudden sea withdrawal.
- Move people to high ground, inland evacuation routes, or vertical evacuation structures.
- Keep beaches, ports, bridges, river mouths, and coastal roads closed.
- Do not wait for visual confirmation; the first wave may not be the largest.

2. RESPONSE OPERATIONS
- Stage responders outside inundation zones until wave threat decreases.
- Account for fishing communities, tourists, schools, hospitals, and persons needing assisted evacuation.
- Use sirens, SMS, radio, police vehicles, and community volunteers for last-mile alerts.
- Keep evacuation routes one-way where possible and prioritize pedestrian movement from shorelines.

3. POST-WAVE SAFETY
- Wait for official all-clear before return; waves may continue for many hours.
- Watch for damaged bridges, contaminated water, debris, unstable buildings, and electrical hazards.
- Search priority areas: collapsed homes, boats, marinas, beaches, and isolated coastal pockets.

4. RELIEF AND RECOVERY
- Provide shelter, drinking water, sanitation, family reunification, and psychosocial support.
- Track missing persons and coordinate marine search with coast guard and local fishers.
- Inspect wells, water networks, roads, and ports before resuming normal operations.
