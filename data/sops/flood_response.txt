FLOOD EMERGENCY RESPONSE AND EVACUATION PROTOCOL
National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) — Standard Operating Procedure

1. PRE-FLOOD PREPAREDNESS (Warning Phase)
- Monitor IMD flood bulletins and CWC river level data continuously.
- Issue flood warning when river levels cross Warning Level (WL) threshold.
- Activate flood control rooms at state and district levels.
- Pre-position rescue boats, life jackets, and inflatable rafts at identified staging areas.
- Alert NDRF and SDRF battalions for potential deployment.
- Issue evacuation advisories to low-lying areas via SMS, loudspeakers, and local media.

2. EVACUATION PROTOCOL
- Begin mandatory evacuation when water level crosses Danger Level (DL).
- Prioritize evacuation: hospitals, elderly care homes, schools, disability centers.
- Deploy rescue boats and amphibious vehicles for areas already inundated.
- Establish evacuation corridors — avoid bridges and low-lying roads.
- Use helicopters for rooftop rescues when ground access is cut off.
- Maintain evacuation registry: name, age, address, medical needs, contact number.

3. FLOOD RESCUE OPERATIONS
- Deploy trained swimmers and divers for swift-water rescue.
- Use inflatable boats with outboard motors for moving through flooded streets.
- Set up rope rescue systems for crossing flooded channels.
- Coordinate with Navy and Coast Guard for large-scale riverine/coastal flooding.
- Maintain 24/7 helpline (NDRF: 9711077372, Emergency: 112) for SOS calls.

4. RELIEF CAMP MANAGEMENT
- Set up relief camps on elevated ground above historical flood levels.
- Provide clean drinking water via water tankers and purification tablets.
- Distribute cooked meals (minimum 2 meals/day) and dry ration kits.
- Ensure medical camp with ORS, anti-diarrheal, and anti-venom medicines.
- Separate sanitation and bathing facilities for men and women.
- Conduct twice-daily headcount and maintain camp population register.

5. HEALTH AND SANITATION
- Deploy health surveillance teams to prevent waterborne disease outbreaks.
- Monitor for cholera, typhoid, leptospirosis, and dengue.
- Ensure chlorination of all water sources in flood-affected areas.
- Spray anti-larval chemicals to prevent mosquito breeding.
- Set up animal carcass disposal teams to prevent contamination.

6. INFRASTRUCTURE MANAGEMENT
- Shut down electricity supply to flooded areas immediately.
- Close railway lines and highways with water logging above 30cm.
- Deploy pumping stations to drain water from critical infrastructure.
- Monitor dam spillway operations and coordinate controlled releases.
- Inspect and barricade damaged roads and bridges.

7. POST-FLOOD RECOVERY
- Conduct house-to-house damage assessment after water recedes.
- Restore drinking water supply after testing for contamination.
- Resume electricity only after safety inspection of transformers and lines.
- Provide financial assistance for crop loss, house damage, and livestock loss.
- Begin desilting of drains and water channels to prevent re-flooding.
- Conduct epidemiological surveillance for 4 weeks post-flood.
